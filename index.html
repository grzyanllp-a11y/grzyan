Our Process

Obsessed over
every millimetre.

NOVA BITES began with a simple frustration: most wafer bites collapse into either too much chocolate or too little crunch. We spent two years balancing the ratio — 38% shell, 34% wafer, 28% cocoa-hazelnut cream — until the bite held its shape and still melted exactly on cue.

Every batch is tempered in small runs, cooled on cedar racks, and hand-inspected before it ever reaches a pouch. Nothing about this is automated at scale. That's deliberate.

What's Inside

Four layers. Zero compromise.

Each element is developed on its own, then engineered to work as one bite.

Cocoa Shell

Single-origin dark chocolate, tempered for a clean snap and a glossy, mirror finish.

Artisan Wafer

Seven paper-thin layers, baked fresh daily for a crunch that holds under the chocolate.

Cocoa-Hazelnut Cream

Slow-ground hazelnut praline folded through Madagascar cocoa, whipped to a silk finish.

Roasted Hazelnut

Piedmont hazelnuts, stone-roasted in small batches for a deep, toasted finish.

Ingredients, elevated.

We trace every core ingredient back to the farm, forest or mill it came from — because provenance changes flavour.

01 — Cocoa

São Tomé, West Africa

Fermented and sun-dried on the island's volcanic soil for a deep, fruit-forward cocoa note.

02 — Hazelnut

Piedmont, Italy

The Tonda Gentile variety — smaller, sweeter, and famously difficult to source at scale.

03 — Wafer

Baked In-House

Milled European wheat, baked fresh each morning on cast-iron plates for structure and snap.
